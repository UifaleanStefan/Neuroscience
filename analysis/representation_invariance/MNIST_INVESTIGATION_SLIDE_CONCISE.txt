MNIST ANOMALY: Why Conv-MLP Fails (69% → 36%)

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

THE MYSTERY
• Conv-MLP: 36% vs MLP: 69% accuracy (-33% gap)
• BUT: Separation ratios nearly identical (1.69 vs 1.69)
• BUT: CKA alignment = 0.70 (similar structures)

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

INVESTIGATION REVEALED

❌ NOT the problem:
→ Global separation metrics (identical)
→ Representational structure (CKA = 0.70)

✅ THE REAL PROBLEMS:
→ k-NN accuracy: Conv-MLP only 40% of MLP (0.20 vs 0.59)
→ Clustering quality: Conv-MLP silhouette -0.49 vs MLP -0.19
→ Per-class fragmentation:
  • Digit "1": 93% variance reduction (over-localization)
  • Complex digits (4,5,6,7,9): 10-38% variance increase (fragmentation)

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

ROOT CAUSE
MNIST digits = simple global patterns
→ Conv detects local edges (redundant)
→ MLP head fails to integrate features
→ Result: Fragmented/over-localized per-class representations

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

INSIGHT
Conv-MLP helps when spatial patterns matter (Fashion-MNIST, CIFAR-10)
but hurts on simple global patterns where direct MLP suffices
