MNIST ANOMALY: Why Conv-MLP Underperforms MLP

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

PROBLEM
• Conv-MLP: 36% accuracy vs MLP: 69% accuracy (45% gap!)
• Conv-MLP hurts performance despite similar architectures

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

INVESTIGATION FINDINGS

❌ NOT the problem:
• Separation ratios nearly identical (1.69 vs 1.69)
• Global intra-class variance similar or lower
• CKA alignment = 0.70 (moderate similarity)

✅ THE REAL PROBLEMS:
• k-NN performance: Conv-MLP only 40% of MLP (0.20 vs 0.59)
• Clustering quality: Conv-MLP silhouette -0.49 vs MLP -0.19
• Per-class patterns:
  - Digit "1": 93% variance reduction (extreme over-localization)
  - Complex digits (4,5,6,7,9): 10-38% variance increase (fragmentation)

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

ROOT CAUSE
MNIST digits = simple global patterns
→ Conv filters detect local edges/textures (unnecessary)
→ MLP head fails to integrate local features coherently
→ Result: Fragmented, high-variance per-class representations

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

CONCLUSION
Conv-MLP helps when spatial patterns matter (Fashion-MNIST, CIFAR-10)
but hurts on simple global patterns where direct MLP suffices
